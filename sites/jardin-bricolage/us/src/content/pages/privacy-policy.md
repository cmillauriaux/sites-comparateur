## Data we collect

This site does not directly collect any personal data from visitors. There is no account creation, no form, no comments system.

## Cookies

We do not set any tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. Affiliate links to Amazon and other partners place their own session cookies **only after a click** from this site to the merchant.

- **Amazon Associates** — a 24-hour cookie set by amazon.com after a click on a `?tag=` link. See [Amazon's privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ).
- Additional affiliate networks (CJ Affiliate, Impact, Rakuten) will be listed here once active.

You can clear these cookies at any time through your browser settings.

## California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect, use, or disclose.
- Delete personal information we hold about you.
- Opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information.
- Non-discrimination when exercising these rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us by email.

## Children's privacy (COPPA)

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.
