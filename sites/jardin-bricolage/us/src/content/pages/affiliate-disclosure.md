## How this site is funded

GardenGuide US is an independent publication funded **exclusively by affiliate commissions**. When you click a product link and complete a purchase at the retailer, we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

## Programs we participate in

- **Amazon Services LLC Associates Program** — *As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.* Commission 1–4 % depending on category, 24-hour cookie window.
- Additional networks (CJ Affiliate, Impact, Rakuten Advertising, ShareASale) will be listed here once active.

## FTC compliance — 16 CFR Part 255

In compliance with the Federal Trade Commission's *Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising*, we clearly disclose our affiliate relationship:

- An "Affiliate disclosure" banner appears on every article that contains affiliate links, near the top of the page.
- This dedicated disclosure page describes the programs we participate in and how we earn.
- Outbound affiliate links carry the `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` attributes for search-engine and browser transparency.

## Editorial commitments

- **No pay-for-play.** We are paid on completed purchases, not on the content of our coverage.
- **All claims are sourced.** Every article cites the third-party sources we consulted (independent review outlets, retailer specifications, manufacturer documentation).
- **Calculated, not fabricated, scores.** A test's final score is always the weighted average of per-criterion scores, each anchored in a sourced statement. The weights are declared in the article's frontmatter so they can be audited.
- **Affiliate links flagged.** Every article that contains affiliate links displays the disclosure banner and uses the components and link attributes documented above.
