## How this site is funded

GardenGuide UK is an independent publication funded **exclusively by affiliate commissions**. When you click a product link and complete a purchase at the retailer, we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

## Programmes we participate in

- **Amazon EU Associates Programme** — *As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.* Commission 1–4 % depending on category, 24-hour cookie window.
- Additional networks (Awin UK, Skimlinks) will be listed here once active.

## ASA / CAP compliance

In line with the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and the Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP) guidance on affiliate marketing, we clearly identify pages that contain affiliate links:

- An "Affiliate disclosure" banner appears on every article that contains affiliate links.
- This dedicated disclosure page describes the programmes we participate in and how we earn.
- Outbound affiliate links carry the `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` attributes for search-engine and browser transparency.

We do not present sponsored content as independent editorial.

## Editorial commitments

- **No pay-for-play.** We are paid on completed purchases, not on the content of our coverage.
- **All claims are sourced.** Every article cites the third-party sources we consulted (independent review outlets, retailer specifications, manufacturer documentation).
- **Calculated, not fabricated, scores.** A test's final score is always the weighted average of per-criterion scores, each anchored in a sourced statement. The weights are declared in the article's frontmatter so they can be audited.
- **Affiliate links flagged.** Every article that contains affiliate links displays the disclosure banner and uses the components and link attributes documented above.
