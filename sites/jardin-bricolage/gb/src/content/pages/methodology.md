GardenGuide UK is an **editorial buying guide**. This page explains how our content is produced, what it contains, and — just as importantly — what it does not contain. Transparency about method is the only way to honour the promise of independence.

## What we do

For every product or category we cover, our team:

1. **Picks the keyword** based on buyer-intent signals (search volume, competition, commercial intent).
2. **Compiles reference sources** drawn from a curated whitelist: independent reviewers (Which?, Trusted Reviews, Expert Reviews, TechRadar), retailers (B&Q, Screwfix, Wickes, Amazon UK) and manufacturer spec sheets (Husqvarna UK, DeWalt UK, Bosch DIY UK).
3. **Synthesises** the factual elements that emerge from those sources: measured specs, comparative test rankings, recurring strengths and weaknesses, observed prices, user feedback.
4. **Builds an editorial pick** argued from those elements, with a verdict that takes a clear position.
5. **Scores each product** out of 10 across observable criteria (versatility, ergonomics, build quality, value-for-money, accessory and service availability). The final score is the weighted average of the per-criterion scores, with the weights declared in each article's frontmatter.

## What we don't do

We want to be unambiguous about this. **We do not perform hands-on testing.** We do not own a fleet of mowers, drills, or steam cleaners that we strip down and time. Our content rests on the synthesis of sources that may have run those tests themselves (Which?, Trusted Reviews, etc.), or on the aggregated user feedback published by retailers.

In practical terms:

- When we write that "4.2 bar of pressure tackles caked-on grime in a single pass," that observation comes from a test conducted by one of our sources, not by us.
- When we score a product 8.5/10 on performance, that score is our editorial reading of the published tests and user feedback — not the result of a measurement we performed.
- When two sources contradict each other, we say so explicitly rather than papering over the disagreement.

This stance is honest and matches the model of many online buying guides. It is **not equivalent** to an independent hands-on review.

## Our sources

We rely exclusively on a pre-whitelisted set of sources. For the garden & DIY niche in the United Kingdom, those are currently:

- **Independent reviewers and test publications**: Which?, Trusted Reviews, Expert Reviews, TechRadar.
- **Retailers** (for product specs, user reviews and pricing): B&Q, Screwfix, Wickes, Amazon UK.
- **Manufacturer spec sheets**: Husqvarna UK, DeWalt UK, Bosch DIY UK.

This list evolves. A source that drifts (unmarked advertorial, marketing capture of the editorial side) can be removed at any time.

## Update cycle

Prices shown in product cards are **fetched at the last pipeline run** from the relevant marketplace. The fetch timestamp is recorded. Prices change — always confirm the figure on the retailer's site at purchase time.

Articles are **refreshed periodically**: we re-scrape the sources backing our oldest articles and update the scores or the cited models whenever sources converge on a real change (new model, rankings shift, product withdrawn). Refresh dates appear in the article header.

## Affiliate funding

This site is funded exclusively by affiliate commissions earned when a reader purchases a product after clicking one of our links. The full list of programmes (Amazon Associates UK, etc.) appears on the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) page. Commissions do not change the price you pay and do not steer our editorial choices: a less-commissioned product can be our top pick when it deserves it, and a heavily-commissioned product can be skipped when it doesn't.

## Errors and corrections

If you spot a factual error or an outdated ranking, flag it through our [legal notice](/legal-notice/) page. We correct it promptly and date the correction inside the affected article.

## Limits we acknowledge

- Scores are reasoned editorial opinions, not measurements.
- Prices move constantly; never base a purchase on a price recorded in an article alone.
- Our scope is consumer-grade products available in the United Kingdom; trade-only equipment is not systematically covered.
- We don't cover every model on the market: only those whose sources document enough material for us to defend a position.
