## Data we collect

This site does not directly collect any personal data from visitors. There is no account creation, no form, no comments system.

## Cookies

We do not set any tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. Affiliate links to Amazon and other partners place their own session cookies **only after a click** from this site to the merchant.

- **Amazon EU Associates Programme** — a 24-hour cookie set by amazon.co.uk after a click on a `?tag=` link. See [Amazon's privacy notice](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ).
- Additional affiliate networks (Awin UK, Skimlinks) will be listed here once active.

You can clear these cookies at any time through your browser settings.

## Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

- Be informed about how your personal data is processed.
- Access any personal data we hold about you.
- Have inaccurate personal data corrected.
- Have personal data erased ("right to be forgotten").
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Data portability.

As we do not directly collect personal data, the practical scope of these rights on this site is limited; for any concerns, contact us by email.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
